Advanced key conditions

Advanced conditions replace the simple keyword, without changing dates or subfolder choice. Add up to eight conditions in order, with up to 100 characters per pattern. AND and AND NOT group before OR. Matching ignores ASCII case; % matches zero or more characters and _ matches one.
